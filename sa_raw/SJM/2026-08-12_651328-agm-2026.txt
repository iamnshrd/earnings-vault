## Mark Smucker | CEO, President, and Chair of the Board, The J.M. Smucker Company
Good morning, and welcome to our 2026 annual meeting of shareholders. I'm Mark Smucker, Chief Executive Officer, President, and Chair of the Board of The J.M. Smucker Company. I'd like to start this 2026 annual meeting of shareholders by thanking you, our shareholders, for your continued commitment and investment in The J.M. Smucker Company. Before we get underway, I want to let you know that today's session is being recorded and a replay can be accessed via our corporate website. Also, today's event may contain forward-looking statements that reflect our expectations about the future performance of our business and actual results may differ due to risks and uncertainties. We were pleased with our fiscal year 2026 results as we exceeded the midpoint of our original guidance while effectively navigating a dynamic external environment.
Our performance reflects the strength of our focused strategy and the benefits of our portfolio optimization efforts, which have created a differentiated and complementary portfolio of leading and higher growth brands across attractive categories. In fiscal year 2026, we achieved record net sales of $9.1 billion and delivered new consumer-led innovation reflecting our focus on building brands consumers love. We remained agile and disciplined, effectively managing costs while continuing to invest in our marketing, commercial, and manufacturing capabilities. These actions drove strong cash generation with free cash flow of $1.2 billion, which exceeded our expectations. This strong financial foundation supports our disciplined capital deployment model, including our commitment to growing the dividend over time. Most recently, we increased our dividend for the 25th consecutive fiscal year.
As we enter fiscal year 2027, we are carrying meaningful momentum into the year and will continue to amplify what is working to position the company for sustained growth and long-term shareholder value creation. For fiscal year 2027, we are focused on three priorities. Driving focused organic volume growth across our key platforms, improving profitability and accelerating earnings growth for the company, and maintaining a disciplined approach to capital deployment. With brands consumers love, world-class capabilities, strong leadership, and a talented and passionate workforce, I am confident in our ability to deliver on these priorities. As always, our success is achieved together with all those who support our business and in that spirit, I'd like to welcome the company's board of directors, executive leadership team, and our auditors from Ernst & Young who are with us this morning.
I would also like to take a moment to recognize and welcome Bruce Chung and David Singer who were elected to our board of directors this past fiscal year. We are fortunate to have tremendous talent and expertise across our board to support the company's continued progress in executing our strategy. Bruce and Dave have been great additions to our already strong board, and I'm excited to continue to work with all our directors to advance our organization. As noted in the agenda for the meeting, we will first address our official business of reviewing and voting on the proposals in the proxy materials and then respond to any shareholder questions. Our executive leadership team, the chairs of our board committees, and our independent auditors will be available during the Q&A session to respond to appropriate questions.
Now, I ask our Chief Legal Officer and Secretary, Jeannette Knudsen, to establish that the meeting has been duly called to order and that a quorum is present for the annual meeting.

## Rob Ferguson | Chief Product Supply Officer, The J.M. Smucker Company
Hi, this is Rob Ferguson. Thank you for the question. We are actively managing and monitoring the potential impact of the El NiÃ±o, particularly within our green coffee commodity. The market for green coffee continues to experience volatility. However, the commodity is deflationary relative to the prior year, as Mark mentioned. As we outlined on our recent earnings call, we expect segment profit margins for our coffee segment to return to the high twenties this fiscal year, which contemplates the impact from coffee deflation. It's important to remember that coffee is a pass-through category, and we manage our coffee business through a strategy that demonstrates a balance between responsibly recovering inflationary input costs while providing consumers with options ranging from value to premium. We maintain a flexible structure that allows us to manage cost fluctuations over time, and we have successfully navigated periods of volatility in the past.
We will continue to pull the levers available to us and manage the coffee business as we always have while ensuring the portfolio is positioned for long-term growth.
