## Vlad Tenev | Chairman, CEO, and Co-Founder, Robinhood Markets
Trump Accounts, for those of you that are not familiar, you should know about them. The idea is, a lot of people do not appreciate what compound interest does. If you start with just $1,000 and you add $50 a month, that adds up to hundreds of thousands of dollars, potentially millions by retirement age.
We wanted to make that front and center, and we want to make sure that because the longer you have the opportunity to compound, the larger that end result is, people should start at age zero and not even at 18 or much later. Robinhood is serving as the sole initial broker and trustee of the Trump Accounts in partnership with BNY Mellon and the U.S. Treasury and IRS to administer it.
