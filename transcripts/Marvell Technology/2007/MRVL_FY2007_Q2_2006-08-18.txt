By integrating Bluetooth into our wireless LAN solution, we offer our customer tremendous advantages of much smaller sized, low power, and higher performance. One of the technical challenges in combing Bluetooth and wireless LAN in a single device is that “b” and “g “wireless LAN radio and the Bluetooth radio share the same frequency and accordingly are subject to interference.
